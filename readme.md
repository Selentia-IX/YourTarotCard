# The Arcanum: Which Tarot Card Are You?

**Take the reading: https://Selentia-IX.github.io/YourTarotCard/**

A sixty-question reading that matches you to one of the 22 Major Arcana, then gives you a full portrait of your
card: who you are, your nine traits, your gifts and shadows, how you live, love, work and grow, your shadow card,
and how you match with a friend.

## What's inside

- **Six chapters, sixty questions.** Agree-or-disagree statements and "what would you do" scenarios, written to tell
  apart the cards people most often confuse.
- **Twenty-two familiars.** Every card has its own drawn mascot, from Pip the fox (the Fool) to Orbis the
  whale (the World). They circle the Arcanum, the three-eyed being at the centre of the deck, and they react to
  your answers as you go.
- **A full card profile.** Essence, the card in Jungian and personality-psychology terms, lifestyle and small daily
  rituals, love, friendship, work, growth, upright and reversed meanings, and kindred and opposite cards.
- **Your shadow card.** The card you sit furthest from, and what it can teach you.
- **Compatibility.** Paste a friend's result link to compare your nine traits.
- **Save and share.** Download your card as a story-sized image, or copy a link that shows your card in chat previews.
- **Draw a card.** The Arcanum will turn one over for you whenever you ask.

## How it reads you

Every card is scored on nine traits. Five follow the Big Five, the most tested model in personality psychology.
Four carry what tarot cares about and trait tests usually ignore: instinct, will, tradition, and how you meet
endings. Your answers build the same nine-trait profile, and the reading finds the card whose shape is closest
to yours. You see the exact match percentage and the runners-up, so if another card fits you better, trust
yourself over the score.

This is a reading for reflection, not prediction. Tarot is a symbolic language and personality psychology is a
science of averages. Neither knows you better than you do.
